How deal verification works
Pricing, honestly

Price & value, without the fog

Sticker prices hide the real decision: billing terms, per-person math, and what free actually gets you.

Billing terms change everything

The same product can cost 2–3× more month-to-month than on annual or two-year billing. We show the term next to every price.

Per-person beats per-plan

A $5.99/mo family plan for 6 people ($0.92/person at 1Password regular pricing) can beat a $3.99 individual plan per head. We do the math on use-case pages.

Free tiers are not equal

Bitwarden's free tier is unlimited in passwords and devices; NordPass's free tier limits you to one device. 'Free' needs reading.

First-year promos revert

1Password's $2.99/mo is a first-year rate for new customers; year two is $3.99/mo. Deals pages that hide the regular price aren't deals pages.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Only questions with real search demand and real stakes — answered in full on the linked pages.

What is the best password manager in 2026?
There is no single best — there is best-for-you. Bitwarden is our value and free-tier pick (unlimited free, Premium $19.80/yr); 1Password is our polish pick (Individual $3.99/mo regular). The full situation-by-situation breakdown is in Best Password Managers.
Are password managers safe to use?
Reputable password managers use zero-knowledge encryption, meaning the provider cannot read your vault. The bigger risks are a weak master password and phishing — which features like Watchtower and passkey support address. We cover the trade-offs in each review.
Is a password manager better than browser autofill?
For anything you care about, yes: dedicated managers encrypt vaults with your master password, work across all browsers and apps, detect breached credentials, and support passkeys. Browser autofill is convenient but tied to one browser and does not warn you about reused or breached passwords. Full analysis in our guide.
How much should I pay for a password manager?
Nothing, if Bitwarden's free tier fits you: it is unlimited in passwords and devices. If you want breach alerts, an integrated 2FA authenticator, and encrypted attachments, Bitwarden Premium is $19.80/yr and NordPass's paid tiers run cheaper on multi-year terms. 1Password costs more ($3.99/mo regular) and is paid-only.
