How We Evaluate

The honest preamble

Most 'methodology' pages describe a laboratory. Here is ours: we have not conducted hands-on laboratory testing. ReadMeHub reviews are research-based — built from verified vendor documentation, official pricing pages, published security audits, and consistent independent evidence, each fact dated. Where we characterize user experience, we say so as evidence-based assessment, not personal testing. We think a methodology you can actually audit beats a fictional one.

Evaluation criteria

Six criteria, in priority order:

  • 1. Security architecture — encryption approach, zero-knowledge design, audit history (as documented by vendors and auditors).
  • 2. Price & value — verified pricing across billing terms, per-person math for plans, free-tier honesty. Prices must be verified at official sources to appear in tables.
  • 3. Trust & transparency — open source status, jurisdiction, breach history, corporate transparency.
  • 4. Ease of use — onboarding friction and cross-platform consistency, assessed from documented evidence and labeled as such.
  • 5. Feature completeness — the features that carry real decisions: passkeys, 2FA integration, sharing, recovery, breach monitoring.
  • 6. Recovery & sharing — emergency access, family controls, inheritance: where real life actually tests a product.

Evidence rules

  • Prices: verified at the official checkout/pricing page, with a 'Last verified' date on the page. If a figure can't be verified (dynamic pricing), we publish a labeled range or omit the number and link the source.
  • Claims: vendor documentation or ≥2 independent corroborating sources; vendor-only claims are framed as vendor-reported.
  • Ratings: none, until a genuine scoring methodology with real evaluation exists. Qualitative verdicts only.
  • Testing: never implied. If we test hands-on in the future, pages will say exactly what was tested, when, and by whom.
  • Deals: VERIFIED, OFFICIAL, UNVERIFIED, or NONE — with check dates; unverified offers never display amounts.

Update policy

Money pages carry a verification date and are re-checked on a ≤45-day cycle (30 days for deals). Material changes are noted on the page. If a page has gone stale past its cycle, our QA flags it internally — and you can flag it too.

What we refuse to do

Fabricate ratings, reviews, or testing. Invent coupon codes. Manufacture urgency. Let commissions order a ranking. Publish prices we can't verify. Create doorway pages. These rules cost us short-term revenue; they are the product.
