Editorial Policy

Independence

ReadMeHub's editorial conclusions are set by our published criteria, not by commercial relationships. Vendors cannot pay for placement, review changes, or removal of criticism. Where we participate in affiliate programs, that participation never determines verdicts — our criteria and their order are public on How We Evaluate.

Sourcing standards

  • Primary sources first: official pricing pages, vendor documentation, published audits.
  • Corroboration: secondary claims require at least two independent sources.
  • Dating: every money page carries last-verified and last-updated dates.
  • Attribution: vendor-reported facts are framed as such.
  • An evidence ledger records source, date, and status for load-bearing facts.

Writing standards

  • Verdict first: every commercial page answers the user's decision in the first screen.
  • Balance is structural: every review includes 'not ideal for' and limitations; every comparison includes choose-if lists for both sides.
  • No inflated word counts: every paragraph must earn its place.
  • Plain language over jargon; tables where they aid decisions, not decoration.

Corrections

Errors are fixed visibly: we publish the correction with its date on the affected page, and material corrections are noted in the page's update history. Report errors to editorial@readmehub.com. We correct fast and say what changed — credibility compounds only if repairs are visible.

Updates & freshness

Pricing is re-verified on a ≤45-day cycle; deals on a 30-day cycle with automatic status degradation if missed. Pages display their verification dates so freshness is checkable, not claimed.

AI use

We use tooling for research assistance and QA. Every published claim is verified against sources by editors, and editorial responsibility for every page is ours. Fabricated or unverified AI-generated claims are removed on discovery.
