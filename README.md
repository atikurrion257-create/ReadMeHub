## 🛠 Architectural Overview

### 1. Design System & Tokens
- **Chassis Background**: `#e0e5ec`
- **Panel Foreground**: `#f0f2f5`
- **Muted Recessed**: `#d1d9e6`
- **Text Primary**: `#2d3436`
- **Text Muted**: `#4a5568`
- **Safety Orange Accent**: `#ff4757`
- **Dark Technical Surfaces**: `#202628` / `#1a2022` / `#2d3436`
- **Typography Scale**: `Inter` (UI/Headings/Body) & `JetBrains Mono` (Technical displays, metadata, labels, data slots)

### 2. Physical & Skeuomorphic Signature Components
- **3D Hero Growth Console Mockup**: Real-time ranking trend line, AI citation share meter, interactive channel switches, CRT scanlines, and phosphor glow.
- **Pulsing LED Status Indicators**: Real-time status indicators in header, console, and footer (`SYSTEM OPERATIONAL`, `ENGINE v4.2 [LIVE]`).
- **Bolted Modules & Screw Heads**: Radial metallic screw heads placed 12px from card corners with cross-groove etching.
- **Ventilation Slots**: 3 pill-shaped stamped horizontal slots with recessed shadow.
- **Punched-Hole Details**: Top-center punched tags on pricing tier cards.
- **Masking-Tape & Sticker Banners**: Textured cream tape banners with slight angle offsets for dates and categories.
- **Push-Pin Details**: 3D metallic/red push-pins on client testimonials.
- **Physical Connector Pipes**: Animated photon light pulses connecting operational workflow steps.
- **Radar Scope**: Rotating conic gradient radar beam on technical sections.
- **Recessed Metric Readout Modules**: JetBrains Mono hard numbers with verified window tags.

### 3. Proof & Placeholder Discipline (§4)

- `+000% [SAMPLE DATA — REPLACE BEFORE LAUNCH]`
- Grey silhouette logo placeholders
- Modular fields ready for zero-CSS content swapping upon client telemetry verification.
